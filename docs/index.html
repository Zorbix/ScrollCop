Get ScrollCop ↓

  1. Download and unzip

    Download the ZIP above and extract it into a folder you’ll keep on your computer.

  2. Open Chrome’s extensions page

    Enter chrome://extensions in the address bar, then enable Developer mode in the top right.

  3. Load ScrollCop

    Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

  4. Pin it. Set your boundaries.

    Pin ScrollCop from Chrome’s Extensions menu. Blocking starts enabled. Open the popup to manage your blocklist.

Before you clock in.

Why does ScrollCop ask for website access?

Access lets ScrollCop redirect blocked websites to its own reminder page. The default sites are listed in the extension’s permissions; adding a custom website may prompt you to grant access to that domain.

Will it block websites I already have open?

Open or reload the website after enabling blocking. ScrollCop blocks page navigation; it does not remove embedded social content from other websites.

How do I update or uninstall it?

For an update, replace the files in your installation folder and click Reload on ScrollCop’s card at chrome://extensions. To uninstall, click Remove on that card. Your blocklist is stored locally, so removing the extension clears its settings.
